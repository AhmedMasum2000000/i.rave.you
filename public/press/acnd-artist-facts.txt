SHORT BIOGRAPHY
Ahmed Masum, known as ACND (pronounced "Ascend"), is a producer, DJ and composer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. His work moves through techno, melodic techno, acid and progressive sounds. His projects include Qbic Pulse with Opurbo (অপূর্ব) and the Ash Army & ACND collaboration "Down In Bossa", scheduled for 30 October 2026 via Profi Music.

CREDITS
