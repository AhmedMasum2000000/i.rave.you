Privacy notice

What the site stores and how to contact us.

Website and contact

This notice covers I Rave You at iraveyou.online and its GitHub Pages version. For privacy questions or requests, contact khalidbinalam250@gmail.com.

Saved player preferences

The player uses browser local storage to remember playback position (iry:mix-pos) and whether you have opened the tracklist (iry:tid-seen). These preferences remain on your device until cleared. They are not an advertising profile, and each website address has its own separate storage.

The audio prompt controls playback. It is not a cookie-consent form or agreement to optional tracking. Music may start automatically if your browser permits it; choose “Browse quietly” or pause the player to stop it.

This clears preferences for the current website address. A player open in another tab may save preferences again.

Requests to the hosting service

Loading pages, images, fonts or audio sends normal connection information, such as your IP address, browser information and requested URLs, to the hosting services delivering this site. The site is hosted through Hostinger and GitHub Pages; their infrastructure may process technical logs for delivery, reliability and security under their own policies.

The website code contains no advertising or analytics scripts and does not set tracking cookies. Fonts are served from this website rather than requested from Google Fonts. Hosting providers may use their own technical mechanisms; this notice does not make a blanket claim about every provider’s cookies or log retention.

External services and email

Opening Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, YouTube, pre-save or social links takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices. Those players are not embedded on this site. If you email the contact address, your email address and message are processed using Gmail to handle your request. Send only information needed to explain your concern.

Retention, choices and rights

Local preferences have no automatic expiry. You can clear them here or through your browser settings. Request correspondence may be kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve the matter and meet applicable legal obligations; hosting providers control retention of their technical logs.

You can contact us to ask about information provided in a request, request correction or deletion, or exercise rights available under applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for legal reasons. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or financial information.
