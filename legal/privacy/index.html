Privacy notice

What the site stores, what it measures, and how to contact us.

Website and contact

This notice covers I Rave You at iraveyou.online, and any copy of the site its operators publish. For privacy questions or requests, contact khalidbinalam250@gmail.com.

Saved player preferences

The site uses your browser’s local storage for the player only: where you left each DJ set (iry:mix-pos, and iry:mix-pos: followed by the set’s name for later sets), which set you chose (iry:set) and whether you have opened the tracklist (iry:tid-seen). Your browser’s own history for the tab also remembers how far down each page you had scrolled, so Back returns you to the same place. None of this leaves your device. It is not an advertising profile, and each website address has its own separate storage.

The audio prompt controls playback. It is not a cookie-consent form or agreement to optional tracking. Music may start automatically if your browser permits it; choose “Browse quietly” or pause the player to stop it.

This clears preferences for the current website address. A player open in another tab may save preferences again.

Requests to the hosting service

Loading pages, images, fonts or audio sends normal connection information, such as your IP address, browser information and requested URLs, to the hosting services delivering this site. The site is hosted by Hostinger, with a copy on GitHub Pages; their infrastructure may process technical logs for delivery, reliability and security under their own policies.

The site contains no advertising scripts. Fonts, cover art and the DJ sets are served from this website itself, so browsing and listening make no requests to Google Fonts, music services or other third parties, apart from analytics where described above. Hosting providers may use their own technical mechanisms; this notice does not make a blanket claim about every provider’s cookies or log retention.

External services and email

Opening Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, YouTube, pre-save or social links takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices. Those players are not embedded on this site. If you email the contact address, your email address and message are processed using Gmail to handle your request. Send only information needed to explain your concern.

Retention, choices and rights

Local preferences have no automatic expiry. You can clear them here or through your browser settings. Request correspondence may be kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve the matter and meet applicable legal obligations; hosting providers control retention of their technical logs.

You can contact us to ask about information provided in a request, request correction or deletion, or exercise rights available under applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for legal reasons. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or financial information.
