Copyright & rights requests

A direct way to report a credit or permission concern.

Contact

If you own rights, represent a rights holder, or need a credit corrected, email khalidbinalam250@gmail.com. Suggested subject: I Rave You copyright request.

What to include

  1. Your name, contact email and relationship to the rights holder.
  2. The work or rights concerned and, where available, an original source or evidence of your authority.
  3. The exact website URL and, for the DJ mix, the relevant track title and timestamp.
  4. A description of the concern and the action you are requesting, such as removal, restricted playback or a corrected credit.
  5. A statement that the information is accurate to the best of your knowledge and that you are authorised to make the request.

Do not include passwords, identity documents or unrelated sensitive information.

Review

Specific, credible requests help the site administrator identify the material and review the concern. Content may be restricted or removed while permissions or credits are checked. A credit correction does not, by itself, establish permission to use a recording or composition.

This is a general rights-contact process. It does not claim statutory safe-harbour protection or designation of a registered legal agent. It does not replace formal legal procedures available under applicable law.

Privacy

Request details are used to review and respond to the concern, and may be retained as needed to resolve it and meet legal obligations. See the Privacy notice. Please use this process in good faith.
